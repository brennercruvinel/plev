# the silences app is the fixed screen the generated one is compared against

Registra que o app de silêncios é a tela fixa contra a qual a superfície gerada será comparada no checkpoint b do programa. A decisão separa um modelo puro, um estado semeado com a detecção gravada da sonda e um worker que executa o ffmpeg como processo filho, e só depois que um ator pede. O resultado é uma projeção semântica estável, com o mesmo golden e o mesmo quadro em qualquer máquina, sem nenhum processo externo ao abrir a página.

## context

the thesis' scenario for a generated interface is the video editor
(docs/pub/generative-runtime-thesis.md, section 4): the
person says "cut the silences of this video and let me review the cuts",
a review surface is born; "compare the original with the cuts", the same
surface becomes a comparison; done, it goes. before the surface is
generated (task-69 phase 4b) the capabilities it composes have to exist
in an app, real and projected, and there has to be a fixed screen, drawn
by hand for the same task, to compare the generated one against: the
checkpoint b of the program. phase 4a is that app and that screen, a
page of the showcase's Apps Demo group.

the constraints it had to meet: the official app pattern (state in
structs, retained widgets, one dispatcher, the model changed only in
`execute`, every action reading the state it changes); ffmpeg as a child
process ([adr-0025](../importacao/0025-urnaui-media-frames-in-place-via-ffmpeg-subfile.md)),
never a library; no test of the gate depending on ffmpeg; the showcase
still compiling to wasm; render on demand; and a projection, a headless
frame and a regression baseline that are the same on every machine.

## decision

### the model, pure

`showcase::model::silences`: the video (name, duration), the settings a
detection ran with (threshold in dB, minimum silence in seconds), one
`Cut` per silence with its `Segment` and its `Decision` (pending, apply,
reject), the counts (total, applied, rejected, pending, seconds cut),
`apply_all` (every pending cut is applied; what someone rejected stays
rejected) and the instant shown. a cut's id is minted once per detection
and never reused, so a request aimed at a cut of a detection that has
been replaced names nothing instead of landing on another silence with
the same number. `parse` reads the stderr of `ffmpeg -af silencedetect`:
the `Duration:` line and the `silence_start`, `silence_end` keys anywhere
in a line (the filter's tag carries an address that changes every run),
closes a start without an end at the duration, and ignores the rest.

### the seed

the page opens with the recorded detection of the probe
(`model/silences/probe.stderr`, the real stderr of ffmpeg 9 on the test
video): two silences, both pending, the instant 0 shown. the seed is what
makes the golden projection, the regression baseline and `--render` the
same on every machine, with or without ffmpeg, and it is what makes
`apply_cut`, `reject_cut` and `apply_all` work on wasm, where no process
runs. a real detection replaces it, with new ids.

### ffmpeg only after an actor asks

the worker (`view/apps/silences/worker.rs`) runs one child at a time on a
thread of its own, with a deadline past which the child is killed by its
pid (30 s to generate, 60 s to detect, 20 s for a frame; the form of
`claude-model/src/run.rs`, not a dependency on it): the test video
generated by `lavfi` when it is missing (`testsrc` 320x180 at 10 fps,
a 440 Hz sine muted from 3 to 5 s and from 8 to 10 s, 12 s, written to a
`.part` and moved whole), `silencedetect` over it with the stderr read to
the end, one frame at an instant decoded to png on stdout and loaded
into the image atlas on the worker's thread (the `ImageHandle` crosses
the channel). the program is looked for at the first job, never at rest,
and never in the crate's own tests.

nothing runs when the page opens. the briefing asked for the video to be
generated on open; the headless staging opens the page the same way the
window does, and a child started there would leave the staged frame at
the mercy of the child's timing (120 settle steps against an 80 ms
generation) and write to `/tmp` in a render. the hook that tells the
window apart is the shell's (`connect_git` in `app.rs`), outside this
phase; a `connect_silences` there can generate on open later. so the
first job that needs the video generates it: `detect_silence`, or a
`preview_segment` whose frame is not decoded yet. the video stays at
`std::env::temp_dir()/plev-silences/silences-test.mp4` (162.908 bytes,
deterministic) and is reused; only the system cleans it.

### started, completed in the wake

`detect_silence` answers `Execution::Started` and keeps its request open;
so does a `preview_segment` whose frame is missing (one that is decoded
completes at once). the page's `wake` polls the worker every 100 ms while
a job runs (`Wake::after`), and `settle` ends each request with
`Dispatcher::complete` or `fail`: a detection replaces the cuts and asks
for the frames the screen shows (the instant shown, the middle of each
cut, one job each, cached per instant, failures included so nothing is
asked again in a loop); a frame ends the previews waiting for it. at rest
the page asks for nothing.

### the gallery's one dispatcher

the page is `Semantics + Operable`, and every actor reaches it through
the todo page's dispatcher, lent by `TodoPage::with_dispatcher` as the
Surface page does. the reason is the agent port: `ShowcaseView::add_sink`
adds the port's sink once, to that dispatcher, and the port matches an
act to its stages by `RequestId`; a dispatcher of the page's own would be
unheard by the port and would collide on ids (both number from 1). the
phase 1 resolution of task-69 declined "the port hearing two
dispatchers". the receipts of the silences live where the gallery's
receipts live; the page owns its model, its widgets and its worker.

### the projection

`silences` (region; `cuts`, the keys of the cuts in order, since phase 4b; `range`), `silences.video` (media:
`file`, `duration`, `shown`), `silences.threshold` and
`silences.min_duration` (`Slider::project`: `value`, `set_value`,
`increment`, `decrement`), `silences.detect` (button: `detect_silence`,
reading the cuts and both values, disabled while the worker is busy),
`silences.cut[<id>]` (list item named by its range: `start`, `end`,
`duration`, `decision`; `preview_segment` reading `silences.video:shown`,
`apply_cut` and `reject_cut` reading `decision`, each disabled when it
is the decision already), `silences.counts` (status), `silences.apply_all`
(button: `apply_all` reading `silences.counts:pending`) and
`silences.status` (`message`, `busy`). the apply and reject buttons of a
row are the item's affordance, not nodes of their own, so a generated
surface binds a button to `silences.cut[i] apply_cut` the way the todo's
binds to `todo.item[i] toggle`. the sliders' drag stays with the widget,
as typing stays with a field; the model keeps the settings the cuts were
detected with, and the status says when the sliders drifted from them.

### the screen

two cards on top and one under. the video card: the frame at the instant
shown (fitted, never scaled up), the clock over it, and the timeline band
with every silence marked by its decision (amber to decide, red for a
stretch that goes, faint for one that stays) and the playhead at the
instant shown; a mark previews its cut. the detection card: the two
sliders with their values, the detect button, the status. the cuts card:
the counts as four readouts, apply all, and one row per silence with its
frame (or its instant on a dark tile until the frame is decoded), its
range and length, its decision as a badge, and the two buttons; the row
of the cut that holds the instant shown carries the accent. narrower than
two cards the cards stack, and a row too narrow to hold the texts and the
controls on one line puts the controls under the texts. every size comes
from the theme's tokens or from measured text; the page grows and the
gallery scrolls it.

## consequences

- the checkpoint b compares the surface phase 4b generates against this
  page: the same capabilities, the same dispatcher, the same test video.
- the projection golden (`rust/plevir/tests/fixtures/uigallery-silences.snapshot.json`)
  is the registry the generated surface verifies against, kept equal to
  the app by test; the regression baselines (`silences@core@1280x800`,
  `silences@hoff@1280x800`) hold the page at rest.
- measured on this machine (apple m4, ffmpeg 9.0.1): a detection 28 to
  42 ms, 128 ms when the job also checks the video, 194 ms when it also
  generates it; a frame 26 to 30 ms; the request ends within 10 ms of
  the answer (the poll).
- the port and the screen reader operate the page through the gallery's
  dispatcher; the receipts of silences requests are in the todo page's
  dispatcher, numbered with the gallery's.

## alternatives

- gerar o vídeo ao abrir a página, como pedia o briefing: perdeu. Um processo filho iniciado na abertura deixaria o quadro staged à mercê do tempo dele e escreveria em `/tmp` num render (`## decision`, “ffmpeg only after an actor asks”).
- ffmpeg como biblioteca: perdeu desde o contexto. A restrição era processo filho, e nenhum teste do gate pode depender de ffmpeg.
- despachante próprio da página: perdeu. O port soma um sink ao despachante da galeria e casa atos por `RequestId`; um despachante próprio ficaria sem ouvinte e colidiria nos ids (`## decision`, “the gallery's one dispatcher”).
- segundo bind para os botões de cada linha: perdeu. A linha é o nó, e os botões são a affordance dela (`### the projection`).
- reaproveitar o id de um corte entre detecções: rejeitado. Um pedido dirigido a um corte de detecção substituída atingiria outro silêncio com o mesmo número (`### the model, pure`, `## avoid`).
- exportar o vídeo cortado nesta fase: adiado. Aplicar é uma decisão no modelo; a exportação real, com ffmpeg, fica para o checkpoint, se ele pedir (`## what phase 4b needs`).

## what phase 4b needs

- an image component in the IR's vocabulary (`media` was left out of the
  v1 in phase 1): a node that shows a frame by an instant of a media node
  (`silences.video:shown`, or a cut's `start`) and a thumbnail per item
  of a `repeat` over `silences:cuts`; the page decodes a frame per
  instant, so the surface asks for instants, never for bytes.
- the surface of the review binds: the counts (`silences.counts:*`), the
  threshold (`silences.threshold` with `set_value`), the detect button
  (`detect_silence`), a `repeat` over `silences:cuts` with each item's
  frame, range, `decision`, `apply_cut` and `reject_cut`, and
  `apply_all`. the proposer must be told the ids are the app's (the 3b-ii
  handoff: the Claude names a template with the app's ids).
- the comparison side by side needs a second media view over the same
  video at another instant (the original and the result), and the
  differences on the timeline are the applied cuts (`decision` apply): no
  new capability, a second reading of `silences:cuts`.
- cutting the video for real (an `export` capability running ffmpeg with
  the applied segments removed) is not here; applying is a decision in
  the model. the checkpoint may ask for it.
- a `connect_silences` in the shell, if the video should be generated on
  open in the window.

## avoid

- running ffmpeg when the page opens or in a frame: the staged frame and
  the baseline would depend on a child's timing.
- a dispatcher of the page's own while the port adds one sink and
  matches by `RequestId`.
- a second bind for a row's buttons: the row is the node, the buttons are
  its affordance.
- reusing a cut's id across detections.

## addendum, 2026-09-24: what phase 4b added to the projection (accepted)

phase 4b lists `silences:cuts` by the cuts' keys (`"1"`, `"2"`), as
`todo:items` does, so a `repeat` names its instances by the key and the
rules find the list by structure. the app also projects a frame ref per
frame it can decode: `silences.video:original` (the instant shown) and
`silences.video:result` (the end of an applied cut holding it), and
`silences.cut[<id>]:frame` (the middle of the cut), each a text like
`silences.video@4.017052`; an `image` of a generated surface binds its
`source` to one of them and the page decodes the frame through
`surface::Frames`. `silences.video:range` carries the span shown.
