# Code of Conduct

plev follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to every space of the project: issues, pull requests, discussions,
reviews and any place where someone speaks for plev.

## In short

- Be kind and patient. People arrive with different backgrounds, languages
  and ways of thinking, and all of them are welcome here.
- Criticize the work, never the person. Say what you see, why it matters and
  what would make it better.
- Assume good faith, and ask before you conclude.
- No harassment, insults, sexualized language or imagery, publishing of
  someone's private information, or any conduct that would be inappropriate
  in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Write to **brenner@hoffresearch.com**. Reports are read by the maintainer,
kept confidential, and answered. Say what happened, where, and who was
involved; links and screenshots help.

## Enforcement

The maintainers follow the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines):
a correction, a warning, a temporary ban or a permanent ban, according to the
harm done and whether it repeats.
